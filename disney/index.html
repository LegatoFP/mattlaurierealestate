7 AM booking

Lightning Lane cheat sheet

Be logged in before booking opens. Book the whole stay first, then modify each day for better times.

Booking window

Disney Resort guests can buy/book Lightning Lane 7 days before check-in for the length of stay, up to 14 days. Booking opens at 7 AM Eastern.

Jillian's tactic

Use an atomic clock if you want to be aggressive. Book Multi Pass and Single Pass for the full stay first, then optimize times.

Single Pass rides
  • Magic Kingdom: Seven Dwarfs Mine Train; TRON Lightcycle / Run.
  • Epcot: Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind.
  • Hollywood Studios: Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance.
  • Animal Kingdom: Avatar Flight of Passage.
Multi Pass rules
  • Pick up to 3 rides or experiences in one park before the visit.
  • After redeeming the first one, book another, subject to availability.
  • Multi Pass and Single Pass can be bought in the same transaction.
  • First 3 park-hopping selections must be in one park; later ones can be elsewhere after redemption.
Dining

Food without friction

Mobile ordering and walk-up lists save real time, but they work best when you start early and stay near the restaurant.

Mobile order

Use the Disney app, tap the plus sign, then Order Food. Start 30-45 minutes before you want to eat.

Arrival window

Popular or smaller spots may not have immediate windows. You can place orders hours ahead.

Reservation discipline

If skipping a table-service reservation, cancel it in the app. Disney may charge $10 per guest for no-shows.

Quick app paths
  • In-app chat: Disney app / three-line menu / Chat With Us.
  • MagicMobile: three-line menu / Account / Set Up MagicMobile Entry / add to wallet.
  • Mobile order: plus sign / Order Food / choose restaurant / choose window / pay / tap I'm Here when close.
  • Walk-up list: plus sign / Check Dining Availability / party size / Hungry Now? / Join a Walk-Up List.
  • Digital room key: resort reservation / Unlock Door / hold phone to lock with Bluetooth enabled.

Night plans

Fireworks, parade, and shows

These are the simplest high-value viewing plans from Jillian's notes.

Magic Kingdom: Happily Ever After
Best from the hub in front of Cinderella Castle or along Main Street. Arrive at least 60 minutes early for a good spot. Avoid castle side views. Leaving takes time, so either wait for crowds to thin or watch near the Main Street exit.
Festival of Fantasy Parade
Frontierland and Liberty Square are less crowded than Main Street. Main Street Town Square is good if you want to exit after. The train station second level can be a good seated view if you arrive early.
Epcot: Luminous The Symphony of Us
Most lagoon views work if trees, torches, or poles do not block the waterline or sky. Good areas include the front of World Showcase between gift shops, Japan/Mitsukoshi second story, France bridge, Mexico boat dock/La Hacienda area, and Italy Isola.
Hollywood Studios: Fantasmic
The amphitheater opens up to 90 minutes early. Center seats are best. Dining package gives a reserved section, not assigned seats; still arrive about 30 minutes early. First rows may get wet.
