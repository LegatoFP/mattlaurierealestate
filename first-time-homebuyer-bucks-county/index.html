FHA Loans

The standard first-timer loan. Lower credit score requirements than conventional, lower down payment. Works great in combination with grants.

0% Down

USDA Loans

Yes, zero down — and it's not just for farmland. Parts of Bucks County and the surrounding area qualify. Worth checking if your target area is eligible.

0% Down · No PMI

VA Loans

For veterans and active military. No down payment, no private mortgage insurance. If you've served, this is one of the best loan programs available anywhere.

🔄
Rate Advantage

Assumable Mortgages

Take over a seller's existing low-rate mortgage. Rare but real — and when the stars align, you can lock in a rate that no longer exists in today's market. I actively look for these.

🤝
Closing Cost Help

Seller Concessions & Lender Credits

Negotiation strategies that reduce what you bring to closing. A lot of buyers leave money on the table here by not knowing to ask.

💰
Down Payment Covered

Silent Second Mortgages

A second loan that covers your down payment with no monthly payments and no interest — drops off automatically after a set period. More common than most people know.

Common Questions

How much money do I actually need to buy a home in Bucks County? ▾
A lot less than you probably think. FHA loans start at 3.5% down — on a $350,000 home that's $12,250, not $70,000. On top of that, PA grants and local programs can cover part or all of that. Many buyers I work with close with under $5,000 out of pocket.
What's the catch with down payment assistance programs? ▾
There's no catch — just eligibility requirements. Things like income limits, first-time buyer status, and the purchase price of the home. That's exactly what the free consult is for: I figure out which programs you may qualify for based on your specific situation.
I have student loans. Can I still qualify to buy a home? ▾
Usually yes. Lenders look at your debt-to-income ratio, not just total debt. A lot of buyers with student loans still qualify — especially with FHA financing or certain grant programs that have more flexible guidelines. Worth a conversation.
Is now a bad time to buy with interest rates where they are? ▾
Rates matter, but they're not the whole picture. A $10K grant or lender credit saves you more upfront than waiting for rates to drop 0.5%. And here's the thing — you can always refinance a rate. You can't refinance the price you paid if home values go up while you're waiting. Timing the market is a losing game.
What does it cost to work with a buyer's agent in PA? ▾
In most transactions, the seller pays the buyer's agent commission — meaning it costs you nothing to have representation. I'll walk you through exactly how this works in our first conversation.
