Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Matt Laurie, REALTOR® with Keller Williams Real Estate ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through mattlaurierealestate.com.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our website forms, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address or area of interest
  • Any additional details you choose to share

How We Use Your Information

Information collected through this website is used to:

  • Respond to your real estate inquiries
  • Provide home value estimates, buyer consultations, and related services
  • Contact you via phone, email, or SMS text message regarding your inquiry
  • Send follow-up communications related to your request

SMS Text Message Communications

If you submit a website form and check the SMS consent box, you agree to receive SMS text messages from Matt Laurie at the phone number you provided. These messages may include responses to your inquiry, home value estimate follow-up, buyer program information, appointment coordination, property details, and related customer care communications.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile information will not be shared, sold, rented, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Email Communications

By providing your email address, you may receive email correspondence related to your inquiry. You may opt out of email communications at any time by replying to any email with "unsubscribe" in the subject line.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Your information may be shared only as necessary to provide real estate services or operate the website and communication tools used to respond to your inquiry.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us directly.
