Common Questions

Should I repair credit before talking to a lender? ▼
If you already know credit may be an issue, yes. A lender can tell you where you stand, but cleaning up credit first may improve the options available when you apply.
Can Trinity guarantee a specific score increase? ▼
No. Credit repair depends on your report, account history, documentation, and creditor responses. Be careful with anyone who promises a specific score jump or exact timeline.
What kind of credit issues can they review? ▼
Common issues include inaccurate reporting, collections, late payments, high utilization, old accounts, and credit habits that may be dragging down your profile.
Does this replace mortgage pre-approval? ▼
No. This is the credit prep step. Once your credit is in a better position, Matt can help you move into lender conversations and home search planning.
Do I have to use Trinity to work with Matt? ▼
No. You can work with whoever you choose. This page is here so buyers who need credit help have a clean starting point with someone Matt can point them to.
